# SAFE Terms Worksheet (educational)

> This is a plain-English worksheet to help you understand a SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future
> Equity) before you sign one. It is **NOT** a SAFE and **NOT** legal advice. Use the official,
> current instrument from Y Combinator (ycombinator.com/documents) and have a lawyer review any
> real financing.

A SAFE is a promise: an investor gives you money now, and in exchange gets equity later — when you
raise a priced round. The key terms decide *how much* equity.

## Terms to nail down
| Term | What it means | Your number |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Amount | Cash the investor puts in now |  |
| Valuation cap | Max company valuation used to convert their money to shares (lower cap = more shares for them) |  |
| Discount | % discount vs the next round's price (e.g. 20%) |  |
| Post- vs pre-money | Post-money SAFEs (YC's current standard) fix the investor's % more predictably — model both | post / pre |
| MFN ("most favored nation") | Investor can adopt better terms you later give someone else | yes / no |
| Pro-rata | Right to invest again later to keep their % | yes / no |

## Do the arithmetic (post-money SAFE)
- Investor ownership ≈ Amount ÷ (Post-money cap).
- Example: $100,000 on a $5,000,000 post-money cap ≈ 2.0%.
- Stack every SAFE together **before** you agree to the next one — they all convert and dilute you.

## Before you sign
- [ ] I modeled how ALL outstanding SAFEs convert in a priced round (use the cap-table template).
- [ ] I know my post-conversion ownership under both the cap and the discount (whichever is better for the investor applies).
- [ ] A lawyer reviewed the actual document.
